Shame is...

I always thought jealousy is a nasty sin But when I look now within It’s not green I can see There’s purple within thee Are you jealous you have not not met me? What is a shame, a regret? Of having done what you now think is bad For you, your image of yourself, Your place in the society What brought you to me Is what paints the cheeks purple You relinquish the ties, the cripple I made you by association In the eyes of your population You want to clean defamation You kick, you spittle They laugh, but laugh is brittle So easy the tables turn You need to make me burn Is conforming so peaceful? If so, I release you From the ties that anchor Your imaginary sinker I see your shameful purple Let’s bring it back full circle I need no man ashamed Of knowing me, the damned For I wear regal purple Of pride that’s very simple Lifted by many people That call me a friend, you sheeple

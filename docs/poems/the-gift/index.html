The gift

It was time, you knew Fearful eyes, too blue You held me tight With all your might You wouldn't let me go My newborn son wept But the hand clasped kept Where did you find strength To keep me at palm's length? Like when I was little And the world felt brittle You were my rock, my shield Cheerleader on every field When fears crept up at night Lullabies showed their might Putting me safe to sleep Kindness and love so deep These last hours we've had Brought your inner child mad With angst of what comes next Leaving me trully perplexed How could I calm your soul? It's never been my role What I could give instead To stave this awful dread Was my presence, my touch Until it was too much For little child to bear I gave all I could spare Hear son this little story Of your great grandma's glory It's true: life chases death The unknown takes thy breath I hope I'll never forget All treasured moments we'd had

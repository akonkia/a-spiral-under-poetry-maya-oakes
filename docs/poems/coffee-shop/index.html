Coffee shop

The door gives a ring, and the lights softly gleam, Disturbed room settles into its pace. The keyboards all click, and the seconds run thick As the strangers drift through the same space Cause we are all here, But we never draw near, Casting shadows in our lone seats. Yes, we do share the air, But we've long stopped to care, About crowds walking out in the streets. Yes, we're all here, But it is all too clear— We don't notice how our life slips There’s a girl by the wall with her cup going cold, Tracing circles she’ll never explain There’s a man with a pen crossing out thoughts too plain, Hoping dreams will not fly out in vain Cause we are all here, But we never draw near, Casting shadows in our lone seats. Yes, we do share the air, But we've long stopped to care, About crowds walking out in the streets. Yes, we're all here, But it is all too clear— We don't notice how our life slips. There’s a laugh in the back, then it fades in the slack, Of the low, steady murmur of day. Then a name’s softly called, like it’s lost in the hall, As if no one remembers the way. Maybe we come here to feel less alone, To hide in the comfort of noise. Maybe we’re hoping that someone'll see through us, Find the cracks in our well-practiced poise. Cause we are all here, But we never draw near Casting shadows in our lone seats. Yes, we do share the air, But we've long stopped to care, About crowds walking out in the streets. Yes, we're all here, But it is all too clear— We don't notice how our life slips. So let's raise the cup, To what we miss: The moments all slipping by. If we’re all here, Then let us be near, More present before our goodbye

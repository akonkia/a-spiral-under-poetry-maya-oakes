Blue canary

Silence was a sign to flee In our ancient history Danger coming, lurking by All the birds just staying shy Take it under, look for coal Like a humanoid mole What you bring to keep you safe? Lone canary sings in cave Forfeit life to save your own Truth we've always fairly known One for many, trolley riddle Can the world sustain this fiddle? Since I cannot shake the feeling We are past the point of healing All the bells and alarms ringing: Do you know the whales stopped singing?

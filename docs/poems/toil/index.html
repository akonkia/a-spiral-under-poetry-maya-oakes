Toil

He wakes up He reaches out Grabs the line Sticky with slime Pulls left leg Then pulls his right Striving to win Uneven fight For in his sleep Eight eyes come quick To drag him back To starting sack He wakes up In disbelief All progress gone No time to grieve He reaches out Grabs the line Pulls left leg Then pulls his right Praying this time He finally might When time runs out He clutches tight Hanging for life Lips pursed in plight But in his sleep Eight eyes come quick To drag him back No crack, no slack He wakes up He reaches out No end in sight No close respite He wants to fly Soar through the sky Abandon toil Break out from coil But in his sleep Eight eyes come quick The plans are foiled Relaunched turmoil If only he would not recoil In fear, in hunger Instead despoil Fight back in broil Then he would see To become free There is a key He ain't no flea Find what's inside Truth self-denied Whose features hide Behind eight eyes?

Brittle

Be cautious While I might seem like I want All the soft words you flaunt Know there’s a risk you are not The one I live for I wear my heart on a sleeve Hoping kind hands will help it forgive Praying for someone who will not leave But I am far from complete An old wound shapes my beat There is a big hole in my heart Neglected, abused, torn apart Looking for love in pillars of trust That have been thinned to dust Alas Be careful Though I may seem tempered and tough My calm is but a quiet bluff When I embrace your kindness, your time I give you a knife without knowing the crime Shatter the brittle shields of my core There lies a pearl I have guarded before Ripe for the picking Longing for a feeling worth sticking Sweet center soft with ache Starved more than it could ever take And though it seems I reach for what You say you’ve got Your praise, your warmth, the light you raise There’s still a risk you cannot face What my heart truly weighs Are you the one for whom I yearn? One who my trust will deserve? Or just someone who was kind Yet undeserving of the find Be aware I might catch a whiff of cheap perfume And mistake it for the real thing Are you ready for that? Am I ready for that? I try to fill my hole with love external People-pleasing for a validation kernel It fills me, completes me But also deletes me Who am I, if I am how your eyes see? A porous sponge in a tears-filled sea Adoration’s waves support my rack Patching holes to withstand the flak But love external Is never eternal See me As I heal my cracks Learning how to love ME back

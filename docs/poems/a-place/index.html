A place

I have no words If I were the wind, I would tousel your hair My laughter singing in your ears If I were the sun, You would feel my kisses Hot on your skin Inquisitive If I were the sand, I would caress your body Share my heat by Holding your hand Tight If I were the sky, I would envelop you Tangle with my horizon If I were a towel I would rub your skin With ardor Dry your crevices Leaving your flesh Gently flushed If I were a cloud, I would let my hair down Fall on you softly Reaching every corner With candor Until my tickles Make you ask For more If I were the sea, I would let you taste my deep waters Send my waves To meet your need Let you sink in me Again and again Like boats at the pier Ebbing and flowing If I were a seagull, You would hear my cry Soaring through the air Taking your prayers To a place that can be Reached only with wings If I were a tree, I would embrace you In my shadow Quivering my leaves Breathing in content While you cool down In bliss Our thirst quenched I have no words For no words exist In this sacred place

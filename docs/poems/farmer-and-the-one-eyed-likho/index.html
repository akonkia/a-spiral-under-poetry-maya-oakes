Farmer and the One-Eyed Likho

In fields of gold and beds of green A sly, thin spirit can be seen For the prosperity of one Invites the curses to be spun With but one burning, watchful eye It might entrance you with its lie For Likho walks where greed takes hold It tempts with ease, it pays in cold One day a farmer raking straws Forgot the old folk whispered laws With envy bid the monster come Opening doors to future glum The mischief started upon that day Dulling of blades, turning axe frail Streak of ill-fate touched men nearby Ominous nature hard to deny Through breaking ladders and stubbed toes His neighbours found the source of woes With one man left unscathed in shame Everyone knew who bore the blame All friends have left, he cursed their name For years he kept their leave aflame He clung to pride but lost his kin And found no peace for all his sin There is one rule the old folk keeps Remember, likho never sleeps Once in it leaves only when bored So, is it something you can afford?

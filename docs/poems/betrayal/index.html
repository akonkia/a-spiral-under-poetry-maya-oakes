Betrayal

I thought I knew you You said you cared You spun words of gold And sunshine It was only a gilded cage Filled with echoes Can you hear me? Screaming out of breath Kneeling in the dust Of broken trust Frantically looking For an answer To this puzzle The bars cut deep While I try to reach My friend Why have you Forsaken me? I put myself Into your hands You killed the flame Scattered ash Nonchalant I could feel your fingers Grabbing Twisting Squeezing Pulling apart My heart I thought you knew me Knew that I’d never blame you Even as I crawl Dragging my broken wings While the gallery of faces Turns against me A poisoned kiss On my sealed lips Will not taint My love Was it worth it? If it was needed I bless you with the ash Anoint you with my blood May it be my Final gift To one who Shared the world With me

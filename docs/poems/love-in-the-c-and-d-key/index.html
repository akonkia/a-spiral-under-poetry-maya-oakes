Love in the C and D key

Here’s to a love that strengthens through years That deepens with laughter and softens through tears A love that wakes each morning anew Finding fresh wonders in all you pursue Through the years you will wander, hand in hand through the gale With a fire that no season, no moment can quell A love where he’ll look, and it’s not age he’ll see but the spark of first moment, where his heart came to be So here’s to the love that is ever resilient A passion that rises, relentless and brilliant May your love blaze strong for all the years to be Ringing loud and true, in the C and D key

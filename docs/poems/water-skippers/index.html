Water skippers

There is no justice Among pond skaters Light of head Light of soul They shrug chains That might pull them down But what is a ripple runner With pockets full of stones It's view obstructed Light dimming Seeing the surface From the other side Dead to the world Falling deep Pressure building around Lack of air Heart pumping Frenetic Suspended Watching the snippets Of its life Play on repeat Stuck in the loop It cannot fix Shadows Lurking in heads Of the others Stitched from their fears It cannot lift rocks Not meant for it Mending the bridges That collapse In the first Cold wind But when it stands on the ground It once feared The only truth remains rock bottom is but a foundation Nothing changes in the Void Beautiful Surface dancer Peel back the weight Let it fall away You belong to the light you yearn toward You were never Made to last immobile Only to outgrow Your limits

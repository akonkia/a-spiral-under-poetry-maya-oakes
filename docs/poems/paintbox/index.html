Autumn · The Philosopher

Paintbox

Tis full of promise, when you bring it home. All pigments are new, cast in pans alone. Plethora of colors, all yours to own. They will not blend, if you dream and postpone. You open the lid, the world feels so wide. Each shade an option, not yet denied. You test with a brush, unsure where to start. The brightest run thin, the darkest feel sharp. Some hues you waste, some you never explore. And space in the box grows less than before. Choices dwindle, pans run dry. Colors mix, time fades the dye. In the sea once you could drown, Now what's left is shades of brown. The box held a world so bright. Echoes serve the failing sight. A short line of muted tone, Final breath in monochrome.

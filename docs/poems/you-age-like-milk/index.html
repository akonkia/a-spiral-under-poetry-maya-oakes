You age like milk

Honey… Who told you that? What? Fuck that twat…! Precious, you are my milk Your skin was soft like silk When I first met You It was more than I could chew You are the substance of my bone Please don’t ever leave me alone For you are the cream to my soup A cold comfort with every scoop I am in through thick and thin So please raise up your chin And when we get old together When our skin turns to leather I will study with affection Your every skin imperfection Every patch, crease and wrinkle Will be worth as much as a dimple And do you know what goes well with cheese? A Graham cracker, beer, a night breeze Through silky smooth or curdly I love you so much, sincerely

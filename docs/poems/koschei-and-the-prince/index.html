Koschei and the Prince

Come close, my friends, and hear this tale Of prince who feared the cold death's stare Of Koschei Deathless he'd been told Of warlock living in mountains old Who through his studies learned the spell To silence forever the final bell No gifts, no vows could sway Koschei To share the words keeping death away So prince kept chasing what one can't buy Through rivers frozen and cliffs too high The needle holding Koschei’s soul Was hidden away, a futile goal Days turned to nights, year chased by year The prince searched on, driven by his fear No mortal hand could claim the prize Though he sought it with the desperate eyes While prince grew weary, still he tried Life slipping past him, flow swift and wide So hear this tale and heed the sign Time wasted in vain is life’s own crime

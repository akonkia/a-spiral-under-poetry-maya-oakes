Twelve Plates on Christmas Eve

In quiet movements we set the scene With golden leaves and mistletoe green Eve honours the toils of months gone by The waning year's feast with a warm goodbye Each dish, a meaning, each omen a tell The mundane clock turned wishing well Our toasts warm hearts like winter wine The whispered hopes for life sublime So lay out twelve plates in preparation For they bring life's joy in combination The first plate holds a tender broth, still warm and softly stirred A healing draught where kindness lives in every spoken word We drink and feel our guarded fears dissolve like morning frost And find in gentle honesty no sip is ever lost The second plate brings pastries light filled rich with thought’s delight Each piece a lantern-glow that brights a gloomy starless night We savour wonder bite by bite where our musings meet And feel the mind grow gentler, calm, and finally, complete The third plate pours a spiced red wine that warms the frozen air A whiff of closeness blooming slow beneath the candle’s stare Its sweetness draws our breaths to blend, our pulses to align A heat that burns through winter’s cold in steady, sacred sign The fourth plate serves a moonlit glaze, a silver winter sauce A taste of beauty captured soft on frost’s enchanted gloss Together, quiet, side by side, we savour sight serene A shared amazement deepening all we hold closely between The fifth plate sets a gingered dough, a dream for hands to shape Where sugared visions rise and swell in sweet, inventive drape We mold our hopes in playful crumbs that sparkle as they bake A shared creation proving love is forged in what we make The sixth plate gathers sugared snow that shimmers as light incites A dish that dances on the tongue with merry, sweet invite We taste its laughter as we spin through games that never tire A joy that keeps our spirits young beside the winter fire The seventh plate holds humble bread, well-kneaded, firm, and true A daily loaf whose simple crust is shaped by me and you Its steady grain recalls the ways our quiet labours blend A testament that shared small tasks make love endure and mend The eighth plate lays deep evergreen, a herb of vow and will Its grounded scent a whisper our roots are growing still Its fragrance whispers gently of the faith we pledge each day A steady strength that guides our hearts along love’s chosen way The ninth plate pours warm cocoa drink, a cup where secrets glide A quiet drink that asks the truth that dwells with us inside We sip our hopes and fears alike, our words both soft and brave And find in shared confession all the closeness language gave The tenth plate steeps a star-lit tea, a brew of hush and grace A warmth that gathers round the soul in slow and sacred trace We sip the night’s deep reverence and feel our souls align A solemn peace that binds our hearts within a realm divine Eleventh plate blends bitter sweet, a seasoned trial's blend A flavourborn of storms endured and sorrows learned to fend We taste how hardship, faced as one, turns iron into gold And know resilience ripens love when winter winds grow cold The twelfth plate brings a lively spice, sharp truth upon the tongue A tang that stings yet teaches how our discord keeps us young For through its bite forgiveness blooms, restoring warmth and grace And shows how honest quarrels carve more room for love’s embrace Now all twelve plates in candle-glow together softly weave A feast of intimacies shared upon this Christmas Eve Each taste becomes a blessing set to carry through the year A gentle path to guide our hearts when winter’s storms grow clear

Map I: Eulogy to Unborn

Fresh of the cart, he picks a stick. Eyes scan the view, no movement tricks. His ink is ash, sketches are quick. Dust settles slowly on yellowed bricks. He marks the roads, which violent gust Turned into holes, in sudden blast. He ticks the homes, whose loss of trust Banned inhabitants to barren crust. Dawn brings the light, noon brings the heat. Don't let the calm force mind to cheat. Playgrounds are empty, no happy feet Run laughing wildly, to their heart beat. Dusk throws the shadows, night - welcome breeze. From rubble pile, wind T-shirt frees. A memoir found of life at ease, The smallest handprint makes his step freeze. No border guards, no marching men Will read the maps he draws for them. But still he marks, with measured hand, The vanished paths of ravaged sand. The map folds once, then once again— A coffin big enough for ten.

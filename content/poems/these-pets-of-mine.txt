It keeps me up at night
It makes me reach for more
It makes me struggle
I have an anger
It goes everywhere with rage
I keep them penned up
Shackled, tamed to be productive
Drive is what they call it
It keeps me up at night
It makes me reach for more
It makes me struggle

It burns with all-consuming fire
No pots breaking, no red crayons can quench this desire

Had I lived in some place other
Had I chose a time another
This fire would push me to barricades
It would make me smash skulls with a blade
Protest fervently worthy causes
Change the world with bloody gauzes

Now it simmers on the edges of my vision
Until it points to next target with precision

I cannot starve them.
Will they leave if I submit?
When they go, will I be me?
What will fuel the passion, my plans and ambition?
Can I trade them for a peace of mind, steady living, white fenced prison?
Do I want to?
Do I...
Want not wanting?
Live not living?

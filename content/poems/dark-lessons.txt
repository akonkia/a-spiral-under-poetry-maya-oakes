Do you know of a flower
that grows to darkness
to freezing
tries to wrap itself
around a sharpened edge
clutches tight
while juices fall
draining
to the nought
Why would you then
follow the things
that hurt you
for no healing
returned
Why would you
chase after the one
whose words carve
a new thing
from your flesh
yourself forgotten
dripping
Is that what
we are taught?

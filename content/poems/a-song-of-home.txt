There was a time
I thought I had to leave
Barefooted
A mountain to climb
To see the world was how I've
Understood it

But now when the door
stays  open I don’t go
All quietened
No summit is calling
no distance to prove that I've
Reached the horizon

Light settles around
The doorframe as I hold on to
My memories
My restless elsewhere
has softened, the world I sought for
Grew in me

I am a sum of four.
I am
a thinker,
a tinker,
a reader,
and storyteller.

I am
a child of transition.
My eyes and hair
bear a kiss
of the sun,
a memory of
summer
turning to
autumn.

My freckles and lines
build a map
of my life,
woken by the
golden light
of October,
basked in
the joy,
celebration,
respite,
and appreciation.

I am
a human,
a woman,
a goddess
of fertility,
an alluring alloy
of strength
and fragility.

I am
a philosopher,
an ethnographer,
a scientist,
and an archivist.

I hide
behind the faces
of Svetovit
and Mokosh,
of Perun
and Weles,
keepers of
the old,
harbingers
of the new.

My life
counted not
in years
but cycles.
My superpower:
vitality,
drawn from
the only mother
we know.

I am
a creator,
a labourer,
a rebel,
a protector,
driven by
curiosity,
fear,
shame and
love.
Untamed,
but limited
by freedom
of others
and time.

My tears
and memories
are fossilized
like insects
in amber;
I wear the
weight of
millennia
on my shoulders.

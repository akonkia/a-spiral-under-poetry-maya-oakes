I always loved to watch the water
Once, a wooden well-cover
struck my head
when I leaned into the dark
to glimpse the liquid below
Stolen glances
in puddle reflections,
Wednesdays drowned in chlorine,
a forest walk -
colors fractured in a drop
suspended from a spider’s web
We chased the roar of waterfalls
Hiked for hours
to drink from a mountain stream
One warm day
I led my sister into the first summer rain
how good it felt
to be wet
together
alive
Now water is everywhere
Its charm washed away by abundance
Dull, gray,
tamed in its flood,
thinned in its taste

I float in the ocean
One finger touches the surface
I turn my face to the sun
as heavy anchors
drag me down

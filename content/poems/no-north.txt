I had a plan.
I lost my path.
I hid a map inside my head.
I had a name for every year,
but every road had left me bled.

Don’t even fear what comes this turn.
There is no north left in the needle.
I have been walking toward the gray.
There is no soul within this riddle.
Of promise broken lays empty shell.

Don’t tell me darkness makes stars brighter.
I have stared; they do not care.
It only pushes your vision tighter,
to tunnels so small lungs cannot bear.

All my chances went on living
in the people I might have been.
No tears come when I am grieving,
stifling in prison of my own skin.

I have no plan beyond the hour.
I fear to look too far ahead,
raising my head to have it cower.
I’m tired. Cannot think of else instead.

Taught to try, to be a changer,
simply exhausted by constant battle.
Try to adjust myself to manger.
No point to make this cage to rattle.

Be cattle.

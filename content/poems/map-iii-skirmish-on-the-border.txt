He walks through a meadow
An open map in hand
He traces border stones
guarding the edge of land
His eye stops on a rock
that had been moved to side
He makes a note of this
the guards won't let it slide
He draws a line in soil
with pointy tip of cane
He checks that correct sand
is on each side of plane
For sand in North differs
from the one found in South
It is a fact undoubted
Known to each hungry mouth
The clay looks suspicious
It hides beneath the dirt
Passing through in long coat
He forces soil to flirt
Dust spirals on the wind
with movement just content
and unbeknownst of border
His white beard hides intent

How often do you return
To thoughts of people that burn
Your soul with unbearable pain
 to meet them just once again?

Time carves us like linocut
Leaves traces as deep as a rut
The more influence we gain
The deeper the cuts remain

As soon as our time is up
The linocut starts its breakup
It decays slowly at first
Gives strong prints quenching the thirst

With time the prints loose their edge
Unless the lines get swiftly dredged
New reprints begin to look bleak
And memory of you becomes weak

I wonder sometimes myself
Will my prints be put on a shelf
Are the cuts deepened enough
Strong and crisp, or just too rough

Finally, all we can hope for
Before death closes our life door
Is to forge strong ties with people
Making sure our deeds leave a ripple

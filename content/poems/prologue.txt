Spinning around
A maple tree seed
Touches the ground
And plants their feet
Past clear zenith
No map at hand
Re-work, re-edit
Fates laugh at plans
Hand full of sand
Eyes un-focused
Grasping at strands
Unwritten opus
Through sleepless hours
Night terrors come
Hushed talks of ours
Peace quietly spun
Wind called to witness
Grains random sweep
One life on surface
More paths to weep

The water closes around my toes
gently exploring
as if it hasn’t decided about me yet
I let it settle
let the surface smooth over
the fact that I’m here
There’s a scent rising
salty
but heavier than that
something warm beneath it
that lingers
in the back of my throat
I shift ever so slightly
enough to be noticed
enough to rub the
budding yearning
Inside
I still myself
The waiting stretches
tightens
until my body is listening
for something I cannot name
And then-
a trace
So faint I almost miss it
a brush that could be nothing
except it is
What I want
I freeze
In anticipation
and feel it stay close
not gone
just waiting
for me to reach for it
This time
I don’t pull away
I let my legs sink deeper
Sliding into the water
I shiver
When the cool
Touches my warmth
And with it
a slow pull
tightening at my chest
as if something unseen
has found me
drawing gently
then deeper
a quiet, rhythmic taking
that leaves me
aching into it
Another pass
closer now
no longer hesitant
inquisitive
sliding between
before slipping away
My breath shifts
The pull returns
stronger now
lingering
as if each movement below
is answered above
by that same soft
insistent drawing
Something in me
gives
I stop pretending
it’s accidental
Let the water move how it wants
let whatever lives beneath
find me again
again
each time a little longer
a little less uncertain
closer
tighter
I chase it
Welcome it
I stay open
feeling the contact come
and return
and come back again
until the waiting is gone
and there’s only
the quiet pull
of letting it happen

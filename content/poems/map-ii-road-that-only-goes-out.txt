He marks the track through fruitful lands
Heavy with weight of leaving hands
Its origin—a roofless square
Its end is bare, for none go there

No arrows point the homeward way
No milestones count the backward day
Each bend erases what was near
The ink runs thin, the road unclear

He charts the ditch where tears had dried
The shoes that fell from smaller stride
A necklace lost in mud in haste
When fearful ran away while chased

He draws a camp spot under bridge
With cold stone beds now left besmidged
He traces all of the remains
Scattered en route like empty grains

With careful strokes he draws a home
A place of refuge some call their own
This temporary breathing space
Will not make the past erased

The road grows pale, then disappears
A thread unspooling into years
The map’s last mark is void throughout
The road that only goes out

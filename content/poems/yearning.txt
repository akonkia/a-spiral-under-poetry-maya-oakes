I find you in the silence
Of the home we have built
I sweep the memories off the floor
They have gathered like cat's fur
Don't you know I am allergic?
Every day I scatter breadcrumbs of thoughts leading to you
I let ants take them
But they come back
I blame the spiders
They have mastered the art of deception
Lulling me into state of non-rememberance
Until maggots turn to flies
Greedy bastards
I don't sleep well, listening to the sound of paws
Rats wage war on mice
Since no one comes to collect the bins anymore
I bet on mould, it would bring some stability
To this festering wound of sickness
I know you won't return
I will keep on straightening the shoes
Praying for a flood

It’s getting late
The phone rings
In a dimly lit room
Voice from
the past
opens doors
carefully buried
A happy family
watches TV
downstairs
The upstairs
quiets
listening to the
tremble
on the other
side
Steps resonate
as if
no distance
stood between
Huffing
Rugged breath
Furniture dragged
scrapes the floor
Mind supplants
images
where there is
only blackness
Silence
rings in ears
deafening
broken by a thud
You listen
in disbelief
a movie
replayed in life
but
behind
a dark curtain
More breaths
Taken
in succession
Panicking
A struggle
is what
you imagine
Useless
Powerless
Scared
The distance seems
impossible
A fast response is
required
But how to
end
the lifeline
when everything
hangs
on this moment?
How to
freeze
the time?
Stop
the galloping seconds
hold the clock
I don’t remember
the rest
But I wake up
in the night
Sweaty
Listening
to a dark room
of my imagining
waiting for
the other
shoe
to drop

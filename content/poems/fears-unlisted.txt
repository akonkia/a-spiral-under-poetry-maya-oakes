Waking up
in the middle of the night
walking to the entry
the door is unlocked
someone is in
Steps in the darkness
Looking into the crib
the baby is gone
There is a monster
under the bed
There is a stranger
hiding in the shed
Forgetting the face
of a loved one
Forgetting your own
image
Not being loved
Body not yours
Loosing everything
Disappointing others
Disappointing yourself
Balloons exploding
Spiders flooding
Falling
Failing
Flailing
Dying
Leaving people behind
Leaving no trace
Body rotting
in an empty room
Grave overgrown
cluttered
No tombstone
No memory of
your life
A crippling monster
turned catalyst
Two sides, one coin
Know your poison
Everything is good
in moderation
Did any of those
get your attention?
